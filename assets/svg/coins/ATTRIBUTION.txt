Coin icons
==========
Many color SVGs are from cryptocurrency-icons (https://github.com/spothq/cryptocurrency-icons),
licensed CC0 1.0 Universal: 1inch, aave, ape, arb, ata, atom, avax, axs, base, bat, bch,
bnb, btc, btt, cake, chz, comp, crv, cro, cvc, dai, dash, doge, dot, egld, enj, eos, etc,
eth, fil, ftm, ftt, fun, grt, gusd, hbar, hex, hot, knc, ldo, link, ltc, mana, matic, mkr,
near, omg, op, pepe, rep, sand, shib, sol, trx, tusd, uni, usdc, usdp, usdt-round, wbtc,
xlm, xmr, xrp, yfi, zec, zil.

Additional icons (hbar, cro, ftm, near, axs, cake, ftt, ata, egld) are simple
original geometric marks bundled with Xorro Wallet Payments for catalog coverage.

Additional icons (dai, wbtc, aave, mkr, ldo, crv, comp, ape, shib, pepe, base) are simple
original geometric marks bundled with Xorro Wallet Payments for catalog coverage.

Monogram placeholders (everything else — roughly 83 tickers as of the 187-coin
registry, e.g. scrt, sei, zro, rjv, tlos, usde, ...) are auto-generated,
deterministically-colored circle + ticker-initials SVGs, wholly original and
not derived from any brand's actual logo. They exist so every coin renders as
a real icon tile instead of a plain text tile in the checkout/admin coin
picker; swap any of them for a proper brand mark whenever one becomes
available. Regenerate via scratchpad/gen_monogram_icons.php if the coin list
changes and new tickers need a placeholder.
